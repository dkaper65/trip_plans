Arrival

Flying into Rome

Rome airports (FCO / CIA)
Touchdown in Italy

Welcome to the Eternal City

We land in Rome and settle in before the Tuscany loop begins. Use this evening to shake off the flight, enjoy your first Roman meal, and rest up for Day 1.

Arrival checklist:

  • Airports: Fiumicino (FCO) is the main hub; Ciampino (CIA) serves many budget carriers
  • Leonardo Express train from FCO to Termini (~32 min) or pre-booked car service
  • Check into your Rome hotel and drop bags
  • Easy walk nearby for espresso, pizza al taglio, or gelato
  • Early night if you plan an early start for the drive to Tuscany
Day 1

Rome to Montepulciano

Overnight: Montepulciano
Morning

Rome - Starting Point

Pick up your rental car and begin your journey north through the Italian countryside.

Options to see before leaving Rome:

  • Quick visit to the Colosseum exterior
  • Coffee at a traditional Roman café
  • Stroll through Villa Borghese gardens
  • Early morning at the Spanish Steps
Lunch Stop

Orvieto Cathedral (Duomo)

A stunning Gothic masterpiece perched on a volcanic cliff, featuring incredible frescoes and a breathtaking facade.

Things to see in Orvieto:

  • Orvieto Cathedral - Gothic facade with golden mosaics
  • St. Patrick's Well - 16th century engineering marvel
  • Underground Orvieto - ancient Etruscan caves
  • Torre del Moro - panoramic city views
  • Local ceramics shops and trattorias
Evening

Montepulciano - Your Tuscan Base

Arrive in this charming hilltop town famous for its Vino Nobile wine and Renaissance architecture.

Evening exploration options:

  • Walk along the main street (Corso) to Piazza Grande
  • Visit Palazzo Comunale for sunset views
  • Wine tasting at a local cantina
  • Dinner at a traditional osteria
  • Evening stroll through medieval alleys
Day 2

The Heart of Val d'Orcia

Overnight: Montepulciano
Morning

Pienza - The Renaissance Jewel

A perfectly planned Renaissance town and UNESCO World Heritage site, famous for its pecorino cheese.

Must-see in Pienza:

  • Pienza Cathedral - Renaissance architecture masterpiece
  • Palazzo Piccolomini with its hanging garden
  • Corso Rossellino - the main street lined with cheese shops
  • Pecorino cheese tasting at local producers
  • Viewpoint overlooking Val d'Orcia valley
  • Pieve di Corsignano - Romanesque church just outside town
Afternoon

Montalcino - Wine Capital

Home to the legendary Brunello di Montalcino, one of Italy's finest wines.

Experiences in Montalcino:

  • Brunello wine tasting at renowned wineries
  • Fortezza di Montalcino - medieval fortress with wine bar
  • Panoramic views of the Tuscan countryside
  • Abbey of Sant'Antimo - stunning Romanesque monastery nearby
  • Walk the town walls and medieval streets
  • Visit local enotecas for wine purchases
Evening

Sunset Dinner in Montepulciano

Return to your base for a magical Tuscan sunset and dinner.

Evening activities:

  • Sunset aperitivo with valley views
  • Dinner featuring Chianina beef
  • Live music at local venues (seasonal)
  • Night photography of the illuminated town
Day 3

Siena & Medieval Towers

Overnight: Montepulciano
Morning

Siena - Medieval Masterpiece

Explore one of Italy's most beautiful medieval cities, famous for its shell-shaped Piazza del Campo.

Must-see attractions in Siena:

  • Piazza del Campo - the iconic shell-shaped square
  • Torre del Mangia - climb 400 steps for spectacular views
  • Siena Cathedral (Duomo) - black and white marble masterpiece
  • Piccolomini Library - Renaissance frescoes
  • Basilica of San Domenico
  • Explore the medieval contrade neighborhoods
  • Try panforte and ricciarelli (local sweets)
Afternoon

San Gimignano - Manhattan of the Middle Ages

Famous for its medieval towers that create a unique skyline, this UNESCO site is a step back in time.

Things to experience:

  • Climb Torre Grossa - the tallest tower (54m high)
  • Piazza della Cisterna - beautiful triangular square
  • Collegiate Church with 14th-century frescoes
  • Gelateria Dondoli - award-winning gelato
  • Medieval torture museum (if interested)
  • Vernaccia wine tasting - local white wine
  • Walk the city walls for panoramic views
  • Shop for saffron products (local specialty)
Evening

Final Tuscan Evening

Return to Montepulciano for your last evening in the Tuscan hills.

Evening options:

  • Farewell dinner at a family-run trattoria
  • Final wine tasting of Vino Nobile
  • Moonlit walk through the town
  • Purchase local products to take home

Sunset at the Colosseum

Return to Rome and take a magical sunset walk around the ancient monuments.

Evening activities:

  • Colosseum exterior at golden hour
  • Roman Forum illuminated at dusk
  • Via dei Fori Imperiali walk
  • Dinner in Monti neighborhood
  • Gelato at Piazza Venezia
Day 5

Ancient Rome & City Charm

Overnight: Rome
Morning

Roman Forum & Palatine Hill

Walk through the heart of ancient Rome where emperors once ruled.

Ancient sites to explore:

  • Roman Forum - ancient city center ruins
  • Palatine Hill - birthplace of Rome with imperial palace ruins
  • Temple of Saturn and Vesta
  • Arch of Titus and Constantine
  • House of Augustus with original frescoes
  • Panoramic terrace views over the Forum
  • Circus Maximus (chariot racing venue)
Afternoon

Trastevere - The Charming Neighborhood

Wander through narrow cobblestone streets in Rome's most authentic and bohemian neighborhood.

Trastevere highlights:

  • Santa Maria in Trastevere - stunning basilica with golden mosaics
  • Explore winding medieval streets and hidden piazzas
  • Lunch at a traditional Roman trattoria
  • Orto Botanico - peaceful botanical gardens
  • Villa Farnesina - Renaissance frescoes by Raphael
  • Browse artisan shops and boutiques
  • Janiculum Hill for panoramic city views
Evening

Trevi Fountain Under the Stars

Experience the magic of Rome's most famous fountain illuminated at night.

Evening route suggestions:

  • Trevi Fountain - toss a coin for good luck
  • Pantheon at night - architectural marvel
  • Piazza Navona - baroque square with street artists
  • Campo de' Fiori area for dinner
  • Gelato at Giolitti or Fatamorgana
  • Evening stroll along Via del Corso
Day 6

Vatican & Departure

Departure Day
Morning

Vatican City

Visit the world's smallest country and home to some of humanity's greatest artistic treasures.

Vatican must-sees (choose based on time):

  • St. Peter's Basilica - largest church in the world
  • Climb St. Peter's Dome - 551 steps for incredible views
  • Vatican Museums - vast collection of art and history
  • Sistine Chapel - Michelangelo's masterpiece ceiling
  • Raphael Rooms - Renaissance frescoes
  • Vatican Gardens (book in advance)
  • Castel Sant'Angelo nearby - fortress with rooftop views
Afternoon

Final Italian Moments

Savor the last hours in the Eternal City before departure.

Farewell activities:

  • Traditional Italian espresso at Sant'Eustachio
  • Gelato from Giolitti or Gelateria del Teatro
  • Last-minute souvenir shopping near Piazza di Spagna
  • Quick visit to Piazza del Popolo
  • Light lunch with pasta or pizza
  • Take final photos of your favorite spots
Evening

Fiumicino Airport

Head to the airport for your departure, carrying memories of an unforgettable Italian journey.

Travel tips:

  • Allow 3 hours before international flight
  • Leonardo Express train from Termini (32 minutes)
  • Tax refund desk for VAT refunds if applicable
  • Last Italian coffee at airport café

Wheels up from Rome

After Day 6 in the city and at the airport, our flights home leave from Rome. Same gateways we arrived through—just with fuller hearts and heavier suitcases.

Departure reminders:

  • Double-check flight time, terminal, and airline baggage rules

  • Leonardo Express or taxi from central Rome to FCO—plan around rush hour
  • VAT refund paperwork, if you shopped tax-free
  • One last caffè before boarding—then start dreaming of the next trip
